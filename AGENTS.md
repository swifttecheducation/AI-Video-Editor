# Life-First Business Video Automation Workspace

## Core Brand Guidelines & Visual System
Every video edit, composition, and rendered output in this project MUST strictly follow the Brand Guide defined in `.agents/rules/brand-guide.md`:

1. **Color Palette**:
   - Primary: Warm Ivory (`#F8F5F2`)
   - Accent 1: Wine (`#720000` / `#B91C1C`)
   - Accent 2: Olive (`#798466` / `#9BAA83`)
   - Secondary: Soft Beige (`#D4CABE`)
   - Strictly NO yellow / gold!

2. **Typography**:
   - Heading / Hook: **Alegreya** (Serif)
   - Subtitle & Handwritten elements: **SVN-Chicken Noodle Soup** (Handwriting font)

3. **Subtitle Layout**:
   - Position: Horizontal center, collar/chest level (~1/3 lower screen, `top: ~1160px`).
   - Chunking: 2–4 words max per slice, natural speech rhythm.
   - Design: Open, airy, organic with soft text-shadow. NO enclosing boxes, NO word-by-word karaoke effect.

4. **Audio**:
   - Normalized to **-14 LUFS** (EBU R128 standard).
