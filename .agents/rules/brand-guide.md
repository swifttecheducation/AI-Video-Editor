# BRAND GUIDE & VISUAL SYSTEM RULES: LIFE-FIRST BUSINESS (SIA)

You MUST ALWAYS strictly follow this Brand Guide across all code, animations, video rendering, and assets.

## 1. Color Palette (Strict 4 Colors - NEVER use yellow/gold)
- **Warm Ivory (`#F8F5F2`)**: Primary text color, clean and airy.
- **Wine (`#720000` / Bright `#B91C1C` / `#DC2626`)**: Dominant accent, key hooks, CTA stamps, focus words.
- **Olive (`#798466` / Light `#9BAA83`)**: Secondary accent, bullet markers, positive notes, tags.
- **Soft Beige (`#D4CABE`)**: Muted secondary text, italic subtitles, dividers.
- **Background**: Dark cinematic charcoal (`#07090E` / `#1F1616`) or natural video footage.
- **PROHIBITED**: Mustard yellow, bright gold, saturated neon colors.

## 2. Typography
- **Heading / Hook**: **Alegreya** (Cinematic Editorial Serif).
- **Subtitles & Handwritten Notes**: **SVN-Chicken Noodle Soup** (Vietnamese handwriting font).
- **Secondary Body**: **Be Vietnam Pro** (Clean Sans-Serif).

## 3. Subtitle Layout & Rules (9:16 Vertical Grid)
- **Position**: Lower 1/3 at collar/chest level (`top: ~1160px`). NEVER place over speaker's face.
- **Chunk Size**: Max 2–4 words per slice, appearing with natural speech rhythm.
- **Width**: ~3/4 screen width, clear, legible, centered.
- **Style**: Free, organic text with subtle text-shadow. NEVER put text inside heavy suffocating boxes.
- **NO Karaoke**: Do NOT highlight word-by-word karaoke style. The whole 2-4 word slice appears together.

## 4. Upper Space Layout (From Forehead Up)
- Top bar: Series tag `SERIES: LIFE-FIRST BUSINESS | TẬP 03`.
- Forehead up: Reserved for Big Hooks, staggered progressive handwritten lists, sketch graphics.

## 5. Camera & Motion Dynamics
- Subtle continuous handheld camera drift (pan/tilt/rotation).
- Editorial punch-in zooms (multi-cam effect: 1.04x - 1.10x) at storytelling inflection beats.

## 6. Audio Standard
- Master audio MUST always be normalized to **-14 LUFS (EBU R128)** with true peak at -1.0 dB.
